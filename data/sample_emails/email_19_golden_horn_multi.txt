Dear DesignHaus Aero Team,

I am Maria Gonzalez, Vice President of Engineering at Golden Horn Aviation. We are launching a fleet-wide lavatory and galley modification programme across our Airbus A320 and A321 fleets and would like to request your proposal.

PROGRAMME OVERVIEW

This is a multi-aircraft-type programme covering both lavatory replacement and galley reconfiguration. The primary driver is regulatory compliance for PRM (Persons with Reduced Mobility) accessibility, combined with an operational need to optimise galley layout for improved catering service.

FLEET BREAKDOWN & SCOPE

  A320 Fleet (8 aircraft)
  ─────────────────────────
  Lavatory Modification:
    - Full replacement of 2 existing lavatories with PRM-accessible units
    - Wider entry door, grab bars, accessible sink and faucet controls
    - Vacuum waste system interface updates
    - Fresh water supply line rerouting
    - Structural floor reinforcement at lavatory mounting points

  Galley Modification:
    - 1 forward galley reconfiguration
    - Rearrangement of trolley positions and stowage compartments
    - New insert power supply wiring
    - Water boiler and coffee maker unit replacement

  A321 Fleet (5 aircraft)
  ─────────────────────────
  Lavatory Modification:
    - Full replacement of 3 existing lavatories with PRM-accessible units
    - Same accessibility features as A320 scope
    - Vacuum waste system modifications (including waste tank interface)
    - Fresh water distribution manifold replacement

  Galley Modification:
    - 2 galleys (1 forward + 1 aft) reconfiguration
    - Complete re-layout of galley monuments
    - New trolley lift system in aft galley
    - Electrical and plumbing interface work

COMMON SCOPE (BOTH TYPES)

  - Water system modifications: supply lines, drain lines, fill panels
  - Waste system modifications: vacuum generators, waste tanks, rinse systems
  - Interior panel and monument fairing work
  - Electrical load analysis and circuit breaker updates
  - Weight and balance recalculation
  - Full documentation package (SB, IPC, CDCCL, AMM updates)
  - Cabin crew familiarisation documentation

TIMELINE

  - Phased rollout beginning Q3 2027
  - Proposed sequence: A320 fleet first, then A321
  - Target: 1 aircraft per month during maintenance windows
  - NRE and prototype phase to be completed prior to rollout

PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

  Please include in your proposal:
  - Separate technical and cost proposals for A320 and A321 scopes
  - NRE cost breakdown (engineering, certification, prototyping)
  - Recurring cost per aircraft, broken down by work package
  - Man-hour estimates by discipline (cabin, structural, electrical, plumbing, documentation, PM)
  - Preliminary project schedule
  - Configuration management approach for two aircraft types
  - PRM compliance demonstration methodology
  - Relevant experience with similar lavatory/galley programmes

We are open to a phased proposal approach if you prefer to separate the lavatory and galley work packages.

Please feel free to reach out with any questions. We can arrange a technical workshop at our Istanbul facility to walk through the aircraft and discuss specifics.
