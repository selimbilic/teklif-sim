Dear DesignHaus Aero Proposals Team,

I am Dr. Elena Papadopoulos, Chief Engineer at Aegean Star Aviation. We are preparing a comprehensive cockpit avionics modernisation programme for our Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner fleet and would like to invite DesignHaus Aero to submit a formal quotation.

1. AIRCRAFT & FLEET DETAILS

   - Aircraft type: Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner
   - Engine: General Electric GEnx-1B
   - Fleet size: 4 aircraft (MSN 62401, 62402, 62403, 62404)
   - Current avionics baseline: OEM delivery standard (2019 build)

2. SCOPE OF WORK

   The upgrade programme encompasses the following work packages:

   2.1 Head-Up Display (HUD) Installation
       - Dual HUD system (Captain and First Officer positions)
       - Combiner glass assemblies, overhead mounting structure
       - HUD computer and symbol generator integration
       - Flight guidance coupling to existing autopilot system

   2.2 Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) Integration
       - Class 3 EFB installation (fixed-mount tablets)
       - Integration with aircraft data bus (ARINC 429 / AFDX)
       - Terrain database and electronic chart display capability
       - Power supply and data interface wiring

   2.3 Weather Radar Replacement
       - Removal of existing weather radar unit
       - Installation of new multi-scan phased-array weather radar
       - Radome inspection and modification if required
       - Antenna and receiver/transmitter unit installation

   2.4 Instrument Panel Rewiring
       - Complete rewire of the main instrument panel and glareshield
       - Replacement of obsolete wiring harnesses
       - New circuit breaker panel layout to accommodate added systems
       - EMI/EMC testing and compliance verification

   2.5 Documentation & Certification
       - Full engineering documentation package:
         * Supplemental Type Certificate (STC) application and approval
         * Service Bulletin (SB)
         * Illustrated Parts Catalogue (IPC) updates
         * Aircraft Flight Manual (AFM) supplement
         * Master Minimum Equipment List (MMEL) updates
         * Wiring diagram manual revisions
       - Certification basis: EASA Part 21J (Design Organisation Approval)
       - All compliance demonstration activities (test plans, test reports, conformity inspections)

3. TIMELINE

   - Programme kick-off: June 2027
   - Aircraft delivery interval: 3-month intervals (i.e., Aircraft 1 in June 2027, Aircraft 2 in September 2027, Aircraft 3 in December 2027, Aircraft 4 in March 2028)
   - We expect the NRE phase (engineering, prototyping, certification) to be completed before the first aircraft induction

4. BUDGET

   - Maximum budget: USD 2,000,000 per aircraft (recurring cost, excluding NRE)
   - NRE budget to be proposed separately; we are open to discussion
   - Payment milestones to be agreed upon contract signature

5. CERTIFICATION AUTHORITY

   - Primary certification: EASA Part 21J
   - We will require the STC to be EASA-approved
   - Validation with HCAA (Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority) will be managed on our side, though we may request your support for technical queries

6. PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

   Please include the following in your quotation:

   - Detailed technical proposal with work breakdown structure (WBS)
   - NRE cost breakdown (engineering, certification, tooling, prototyping)
   - Recurring cost per aircraft (labour, materials, documentation)
   - Man-hour estimate per aircraft by discipline
   - Risk assessment and mitigation plan
   - Preliminary project schedule with milestones
   - Team composition and key personnel CVs
   - Relevant experience and reference projects (B787 preferred)
   - Quality assurance and configuration management approach

7. PROPOSAL DEADLINE

   We kindly request your proposal no later than 15 September 2026. We plan to complete the vendor evaluation by end of October 2026 and award the contract in November 2026.
