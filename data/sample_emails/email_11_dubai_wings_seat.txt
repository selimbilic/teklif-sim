Dear DesignHaus Aero Proposals Team,

I hope this message finds you well. My name is Ahmed Al-Rashid and I serve as Vice President of Fleet Planning at Dubai Wings. We have been following your recent A320 interior programs with considerable interest, and I am writing to formally request a proposal for an economy class seat replacement across our Airbus A320-200 fleet.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

We intend to fully replace the existing economy class seating in rows 7 through 31, which currently accommodate 150 passengers in an all-economy configuration. The replacement seat model we have selected is the Recaro CL3710. Each seat unit must be equipped with integrated USB-C charging ports at every passenger position.

SCOPE OF WORK

The scope we envision includes, but is not limited to, the following:

  1. Removal and disposal of existing economy class seat units (rows 7–31)
  2. Supply and installation of Recaro CL3710 seat assemblies with USB-C power modules
  3. Modification and replacement of interior sidewall and ceiling panels in the economy cabin zone to match the refreshed configuration
  4. All necessary structural interface work (seat tracks, tie-down fittings, floor panels)
  5. Full documentation package, including:
     - Service Bulletin (SB)
     - Illustrated Parts Catalogue (IPC) updates
     - Certification Data and Compliance Check List (CDCCL)
  6. Weight and balance report updates
  7. Electrical load analysis for the added USB-C circuits

AIRCRAFT DETAILS

  - Aircraft type: Airbus A320-200
  - Engine: CFM International CFM56-5B
  - Current cabin configuration: 150 PAX, single-class economy

TARGET TIMELINE

We are targeting programme initiation in Q1 2027, with the first aircraft delivery expected no later than end of March 2027. We would appreciate a realistic production schedule in your proposal.

PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

Please include the following in your response:

  - Detailed technical proposal with work breakdown
  - Preliminary engineering cost estimate (NRE and recurring per aircraft)
  - Estimated man-hours per aircraft (broken down by discipline)
  - Lead time for materials and engineering
  - Proposed project milestones

We would be happy to arrange a technical kick-off call at your earliest convenience to discuss the finer details. Please do not hesitate to reach out if you require any additional information from our side.

We look forward to your proposal.
