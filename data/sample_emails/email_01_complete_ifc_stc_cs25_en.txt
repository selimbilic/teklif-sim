Dear AeroDesign Engineering Team,

Flagship Air is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) for a complete Design Organisation Approval (DOA) engineering package to install a Satellite In-Flight Connectivity (IFC) Ku-Band antenna radome across our fleet of 6 Boeing 737-800 aircraft.

Project Specifications:
- Aircraft Model: Boeing 737-800 (CS-25 Commercial Transport Aeroplane)
- Fleet Size: 6 aircraft
- Customer Account: Flagship Air (Flagship Account)
- Safety Assessment: ARP4761 FHA/SSA assigning DAL B (Hazardous) to flight deck power isolation & RF interference mitigation
- Wiring Integration: CS 25.1707 (Subpart H) EWIS compliance analysis required for main power feed and ARINC 429 bus routing

Scope of Work:
1. Fuselage crown structural cutout and external doubler plate stress analysis.
2. Installation of tri-axial satellite antenna, Wireless Access Points (WAPs), and server rack in E&E bay.
3. Full EASA Part 21 Major Change (STC) compliance package (Certification Programme, SB, Structural/EWIS Reports, FHA/SSA, ICA).

Requested Manhour Allocation:
- Cabin Design Engineer: 30 hours
- Structural Engineer: 90 hours
- Avionics Design Engineer: 140 hours
- Certification Engineer: 45 hours
- Project Manager: 30 hours
